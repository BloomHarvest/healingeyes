— Real Questions, Real Answers

Before You Apply

Everything you'd want to ask before committing to the Academy.

When does Cohort 1 start, and what if I miss it? +
Cohort 1 begins July 1, 2026. The application window is open now. If you miss this cohort, the next will open later in 2026 — but capacity is limited and Cohort 1 will fill first. Group Track caps at 8 students. Private Track caps at 2 students.
Why is there an application instead of just letting me buy? +
The Academy is a small-cohort program with limited seats. Samantha personally reads each application to make sure the cohort is filled with people who will do the work. The application protects you (you're not paying $5,000 to be in a room of disengaged students) and protects the program.
Can I switch tracks once I'm accepted? +
Possibly — depending on track availability. If you're accepted into Self-Paced and the Group Track has open seats, you can upgrade by paying the difference. Downgrades aren't refundable but you can apply your tuition to a future cohort if circumstances change.
Do I need to have completed the Foundation Course first? +
No, the Foundation Course is not a prerequisite. The Academy starts from foundational principles and builds to advanced reading work. That said, if you've already completed Foundation Course, you'll move through Module 1 faster.
What if I can't make the weekly calls? +
Group Track calls are recorded — if you miss one, you'll have access to the replay within 24 hours. Private Track sessions are scheduled with you personally, so we can find times that work. If you regularly can't make weekly calls, the Self-Paced track is probably the better fit.
