Before You Begin

Everything you want to know before working with Samantha.

What is iridology and sclerology? +
Iridology reads patterns and markers in the iris. Sclerology reads the white of the eye. Combined, they reveal patterns about the body's systems, stress, and ancestry. They are not diagnostic tools — they're educational mirrors.
Is this a medical diagnosis? +
No. Iridology and sclerology are not diagnostic tools and are not a substitute for medical care. Samantha is an iridologist, not a doctor. The work is educational, observational, and supportive — never prescriptive.
Is Samantha certified? +
Samantha trained under a certified iridologist and chose not to pursue formal certification herself. She's been practicing since 2021 and has read thousands of eyes. Her methodology is her own — built from training, practice, and five years of client work.
How do I send my eye photos? +
After purchasing, you'll receive an email with photo guidelines and a secure upload link. Most clients use their phone camera in good natural light. We provide examples of what makes a good photo.
How long does it take to get everything back to me? +
Snapshots are typically delivered within 3-5 business days. Video Analyses within 5-7 business days. Live Sessions are scheduled at the time of booking.
What's the difference between the Snapshot, Video, and Live Session? +
The Snapshot is a written PDF reading of your eye photos. The Video is a recorded walkthrough where Samantha talks through what she sees. The Live Session is a 60-minute video call with real-time interaction and questions.
Do you offer refunds? +
Digital products and completed readings are non-refundable. Live sessions can be rescheduled ONE TIME with 24-hour notice.
